Your picks help make The Edge better

When you pick a game here, The Edge takes a copy: who you took, and the line and price showing when you took them. It goes with an id — sixteen characters worked out from your licence key.

Your name, your email and your licence key are not sent. The id isn't your name, but The Edge can work out which customer it belongs to.

Picks are used to grade pickers, to improve the model, and The Edge may follow picks from people who are consistently right — which is what that last part is for.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings, and delete everything that's been shared.
